Two places Archivo does not go. Email clients strip webfonts, and PNG/PDF export cannot embed them — so the email templates and the social and print artboards are set in the system stack and sized to it. That is why they look slightly different from this sheet: it is deliberate, not drift.

The rule the whole kit is built on
Picture first, then the words A 72 px pictogram opens every email. If the picture alone does not carry the message, the email is wrong.
Numbers are read by everyone Digits are learned long before prose. The amount is the biggest thing on the page, at 40–52 px.
Shorter copy is not the fix Trimming words helps a slow reader and does nothing for a non-reader. Change the channel — picture, number, colour, voice note.
