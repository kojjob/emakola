Motion — the reveal

The stall opens for business

The mark is a shop, so it should assemble like one — roof up, awning down, posts, counter. A merchant who cannot read the wordmark watches a stall being set up, which is the same sentence in a different language.

Easing

cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1)

The valance alone overshoots — (.3,.9,.4,1.25) — so the cloth settles like cloth.

Transform origins

Valance, posts and front grow from their top. The counter grows from its centre. Roof translates only — never scale a triangle.

Plays once, on first paint

On the canvas it loops every 3.4 s so you can watch it. In production set animation-iteration-count: 1 and drop the hold frames.

Costs nothing to ship

Six inline paths and one stylesheet block. No Lottie, no JSON, no JavaScript, no extra request — which is the only kind of animation a 2G connection forgives.

Reduced motion is the default

Every part is authored in its finished state; the keyframes live inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference. Turn motion off and you get the mark, never a blank box.
