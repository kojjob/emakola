Using the mark

Rules that keep it recognisable

A mark that changes shape every time it appears has to be re-learned every time. For an audience that recognises the picture before the word, consistency is not fussiness — it is the whole mechanism.

Clear space = a quarter of the coin
Makola.io

Nothing enters the dashed box — not a nav item, not a photo edge, not another logo. Measure it as a quarter of the coin’s diameter so it scales with the mark instead of being a fixed pixel number.

Below 24 px the teeth fill in. Use the simplified cut — coin and slit only, eight teeth at most — never a shrunk copy of the full mark.

✕ Never

Stretch or squash it. The coin is a circle — always.

✕ Never

Recolour it. Gold and ink, or one flat colour.

✕ Never

Drop it onto a busy photo. Add an ink scrim first.

✕ Never

Rotate it or add a shadow, bevel or glow.
