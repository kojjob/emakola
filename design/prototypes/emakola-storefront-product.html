Description

The Kente Wrap Dress is a celebration of Ghanaian heritage reimagined for the modern woman. Each piece is handwoven on traditional wooden looms by master artisans in Bonwire, the ancestral home of kente weaving in the Ashanti Region. The intricate patterns tell stories of royalty, wisdom, and cultural pride, passed down through generations of weavers.

Designed with a flattering wrap silhouette, the dress cinches at the waist with a self-tie belt, creating a universally flattering shape. The structured shoulders and midi length make it appropriate for formal occasions, while the lightweight cotton fabric keeps you comfortable in Ghana's warm climate. Pair it with gold accessories for a traditional look, or dress it down with sandals for a casual weekend outing.

Materials & Care
  • 100% handwoven kente cotton
  • Hand wash cold with mild detergent
  • Do not bleach
  • Lay flat to dry in shade
  • Iron on low heat, inside out
  • Store folded, not hung, to preserve fabric integrity
