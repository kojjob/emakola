Description

The Wool Blend Overcoat is a testament to refined outerwear, blending timeless silhouette with contemporary tailoring. Crafted in Italy from a carefully sourced wool-cashmere blend, this piece delivers warmth without bulk, draping elegantly over any ensemble.

Features include a notched lapel collar, hidden button placket for a streamlined look, two front welt pockets, and an interior chest pocket. The half-canvas construction ensures the coat retains its shape beautifully over time.

Designed for a relaxed yet polished fit, this overcoat falls just below the knee and pairs effortlessly with tailored trousers, denim, or layered over knitwear for a complete cold-weather look.
