Pattern A · recommended

One hero, then a rail

Rank 1 keeps the big treatment. Everyone else appears immediately at card size, no swiping required. Scales to 8 or 80 without the layout changing shape.

Pattern C

Keep three, rotate who they are

The hero never grows. A window over featured_rank moves each day, so across a week every featured merchant holds the big slot.

Today Adom Fresh · Nana Yaa · Serwaa Skin
Tomorrow Akosua Hair · Boakye Poultry · Kofi Cuts
Wed Ntoma House · Osu Electricals · Adom Fresh
Thu Serwaa Skin · Kofi Cuts · Nana Yaa
What it costs you The page looks different on every visit, so a shopper cannot go back to the shop they saw yesterday, and merchants cannot predict when their turn lands. Fairest to merchants, hardest on shoppers.
The decision underneath

All three are layout answers to a business question: is Featured earned, bought, or curated? If merchants pay for it, silently dropping rank nine is a refund conversation, and Pattern C is the only fair one. If you curate it, Pattern A is right and the cap can stay at 8.
