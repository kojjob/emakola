The same card with the words taken out Most of these merchants do not read fluently. Cutting the copy shorter does not help a non-reader at all — the only thing that helps is putting the answer in a picture, a colour, a position or a digit. So: cover every word on the card and see what survives. If price, profit, stock, supplier and connection all still come through, the card works for someone who reads nothing.

What survives, and how
Which product
The photograph, at 156px. When the supplier has none, the tote glyph — never an empty grey box a merchant has to decode.
K
Which supplier
One letter, one fixed colour per supplier. Recognised by shape and hue long before the name is read. The green tick on the corner means you already deal with them.
The price
Tag, black, biggest number on the card. Digits are the one kind of text nearly every trader reads fluently — so the money is carried by digits, never by words.
What stays with you
Coins, green, always in a green box under the price. A locked one shows the padlock and a covered amount in the same box — same place, so the difference is the thing you notice.
How much is left
A bar you compare by length. Green or amber only: an offer with nothing left is not discoverable at all, so "out of stock" never reaches this page. The supplier's raw count is never shown — SupplyStockStatus is status-only.
What delivery costs
Pin plus a number. Grey pin and a short chip when the supplier has not quoted one.
