# Emakola — Brand Guidelines

## Identity

- **Name**: Emakola (eh-mah-KOH-lah) — always one word, capital E
- **Mission**: Empowering West African entrepreneurs to build thriving online businesses
- **Vision**: The commerce infrastructure of Africa
- **Values**: Accessibility, Reliability, Community, Growth
- **Personality**: Trustworthy, modern, approachable, empowering

## Color Palette

### Primary
- **Emakola Green**: `#059669` (emerald-600) — Trust, growth, prosperity
- **Emakola Dark**: `#064E3B` (emerald-900) — Depth, authority
- **Emakola Light**: `#D1FAE5` (emerald-100) — Backgrounds

### Secondary
- **Gold**: `#CA8A04` — Success, premium, achievement
- **Coral**: `#F43F5E` — Urgency, sales, alerts
- **Slate**: `#334155` — Text, UI elements

### Neutrals
- **Background**: `#FAFAF9`
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Border**: `#E2E8F0`
- **Text Primary**: `#0F172A`
- **Text Secondary**: `#475569`
- **Text Muted**: `#94A3B8`

## Typography

| Use | Font | Weights | Why |
|-----|------|---------|-----|
| Headings | Inter | 600, 700 | Clean, modern, widely supported |
| Body | Inter | 400, 500 | Excellent readability on all devices |
| Monospace | JetBrains Mono | 400, 500 | Prices, order numbers, codes |

Inter supports Latin Extended — covers French for francophone West Africa expansion.

## Voice & Tone

- **Merchant-facing**: Professional but warm. "We're building this with you."
- **Customer-facing**: Friendly, clear, no jargon.
- **Errors**: Empathetic + actionable. "We couldn't process your payment. Try again or use a different method."
- **Success**: Warm but not over the top. "Order confirmed! You'll receive an SMS shortly."

## Cultural Considerations

- Use local imagery, not Western stock photos
- Respect local naming conventions
- Currency symbols: GH₵ (Ghana), ₦ (Nigeria)
- Phone format: +233 XX XXX XXXX
- Date format: DD/MM/YYYY
- Design for 3G first — no heavy animations or large images by default
- Support both English and local languages in future
