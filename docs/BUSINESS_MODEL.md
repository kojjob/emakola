# Emakola — Business Model

## Revenue Streams

1. **Monthly subscriptions** — recurring platform fee
2. **Transaction fees** — percentage of each sale processed
3. **Premium add-ons** — WhatsApp messaging, extra storage, themes (Phase 2+)

## Unit Economics

**Average Growth merchant:**
- Monthly subscription: GH₵ 49
- Monthly GMV (Gross Merchandise Value): GH₵ 20,000
- Transaction fee (2%): GH₵ 400
- **Monthly revenue per merchant: GH₵ 449**

**Costs per merchant:**
- Infrastructure: ~GH₵ 15/mo
- Payment gateway fees: ~GH₵ 200/mo (passed through)
- SMS/WhatsApp: ~GH₵ 20/mo
- Support: ~GH₵ 30/mo
- **Monthly cost: ~GH₵ 65** (excluding gateway passthrough)
- **Gross margin: ~85%**

**Targets:**
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost): < GH₵ 200
- LTV (Lifetime Value): > GH₵ 5,000 (12+ months retention)
- LTV:CAC ratio: > 3:1
- Monthly churn: < 5%

## Go-to-Market (Ghana)

### Phase 1: Instagram/WhatsApp Merchants
- **Who**: Merchants already selling on Instagram/WhatsApp (thousands in Accra alone)
- **Pain**: Manual order tracking, no proper checkout, no payment integration
- **Pitch**: "Turn your Instagram shop into a real store in 10 minutes"
- **Channel**: Instagram/Facebook ads targeting small business owners in Ghana

### Phase 2: Paystack/Hubtel Merchant Referrals
- Partner with payment providers who have merchant bases
- Co-marketing: "Accept mobile money on your own store"

### Phase 3: Content Marketing
- "How to start selling online in Ghana" (SEO play)
- Merchant success stories
- YouTube tutorials in English + Twi

### Phase 4: Referral Program
- Merchants refer merchants: both get 1 month free
- Affiliate program for influencers
