# Emakola — Competitive Analysis

## Direct Competitors in West Africa

### Paystack Commerce (by Stripe/Paystack)
- **Strengths**: Already has payment infrastructure, brand trust, simple setup
- **Weaknesses**: Very basic store features, no themes, limited customization, no inventory management, no shipping integration, no WhatsApp
- **Our Edge**: Full-featured store builder, themes, WhatsApp integration, local logistics, mobile money as first-class

### Flutterwave Store
- **Strengths**: Pan-African payment coverage, quick setup
- **Weaknesses**: Extremely basic, essentially a payment link with a product page, no real store management
- **Our Edge**: Everything — real product management, customer accounts, order workflows, analytics

### Jumia (Marketplace)
- **Strengths**: Brand recognition, existing logistics network, customer base
- **Weaknesses**: Marketplace model (not SaaS), takes 10-25% commission, merchants don't own their customer relationship
- **Our Edge**: Merchants own their store, brand, customer data. Lower fees. Independence.

### Shopify (International)
- **Strengths**: Best-in-class platform, massive app ecosystem, proven at scale
- **Weaknesses**: Pricing in USD ($39/mo = GH₵ 600+ monthly), no native mobile money, no WhatsApp, no local logistics, not built for West African SME realities
- **Our Edge**: Local pricing (GH₵ 49/mo), native MoMo/WhatsApp/local logistics, mobile-first for 3G, understands the market

### WooCommerce (Self-hosted)
- **Strengths**: Free, flexible, massive plugin ecosystem
- **Weaknesses**: Requires technical knowledge, hosting management, security responsibility, plugin hell, no native mobile money
- **Our Edge**: Managed SaaS (no hosting headaches), native integrations, support in local languages

## Key Differentiators

1. **Mobile Money Native** — Not an afterthought plugin, but the primary checkout method
2. **WhatsApp-First Communication** — Order updates, support, abandoned cart recovery
3. **Local Pricing** — GH₵ 49/mo not $39/mo. Priced for West African SMEs
4. **3G-Optimized** — LiveView architecture means fast loads on slow connections
5. **Cash on Delivery Workflow** — Built-in COD with driver confirmation, not just a payment label
6. **Local Logistics** — Native integration with West African couriers
7. **Multi-Language** — English, French, Twi, Hausa, Yoruba (Phase 4)

## Target Merchant Profile

### Primary: Digital-First Small Merchants (Ghana)
- Selling on Instagram/WhatsApp, want a proper store
- 10-500 products
- GH₵ 5,000 - 50,000 monthly revenue
- Accept mobile money primarily
- Need: simple setup, WhatsApp notifications, MoMo checkout

### Secondary: Growing Brands (Ghana/Nigeria)
- Established brands wanting independence from marketplaces
- 100-5,000 products
- GH₵ 50,000+ monthly revenue
- Need: full store customization, analytics, multi-channel

### Tertiary: Enterprise / Multi-Location
- Large retailers wanting online presence
- Need: API access, custom integrations, dedicated support
