# Makola — Every Feature, Explained in Plain English

*A non-technical guide to everything the platform does. Written so anyone — a market
trader, an investor, a new team member — can understand it without a tech background.*

---

## What Makola is, in one breath

Makola is **"Shopify built for West Africa."** It lets anyone in Ghana (and soon Nigeria)
open a proper online shop in minutes — one that takes **mobile money** (MTN MoMo, Telecel
Cash, AirtelTigo Money), sends customers **WhatsApp and SMS** updates, and works smoothly on
a cheap phone with a weak data connection. Sellers get their own branded storefront;
shoppers get a fast, familiar buying experience; and we (the platform) earn a small fee on
each sale.

Think of it as three connected worlds:

1. **The shoppers' world** — the people buying things.
2. **The sellers' world** — the shop owners running their business.
3. **Our world (the platform)** — the control room where we keep everything safe, fair, and
   profitable.

Plus a **money engine** humming underneath all three.

---

## 1. The Shoppers' World — what a customer experiences

When someone visits a seller's shop (e.g. `kente-kingdom.makola.io`), here's everything they
can do:

- **Browse the shop** — a clean homepage, a full product list, and product pages with photos,
  prices, descriptions, and options (size, colour, etc.).
- **Search and filter by category** — find "dresses" or "spices" without scrolling forever.
- **Add to cart & check out** — a simple, single-page checkout designed for phones.
- **Pay with mobile money or card** — a MoMo prompt pops up on their phone; they approve it and
  the order is placed. Card and "pay on delivery" are options too.
- **Get order updates** — a confirmation page, plus WhatsApp/SMS messages as the order moves
  along.
- **Track their order** — a public tracking page (like tracking a parcel) using just the order
  number.
- **Save things for later** — a **wishlist** of products they like.
- **Follow shops they love** — a **saved stores** list, so favourite sellers are one tap away.
- **Buy digital products** — e-books, music, design files, etc. After paying, they get a secure
  download link (no shipping needed).
- **Create an account** — to see past orders and downloads. They can sign up the normal way or
  **log in with WhatsApp** (just a phone number and a code — no password to forget).
- **Read the shop's story** — every seller can publish an **About** page, **Contact** details,
  an **FAQ**, **store policies** (returns, shipping, privacy), a **blog**, **recipes**, and
  any **custom pages** they want. This makes small shops feel trustworthy and established.

The whole experience is **mobile-first and low-data**: images load lazily, pages are light, and
nothing assumes a fast connection.

## 2. The Sellers' World — running a shop

This is the heart of the product: a complete back office for a shop owner. Everything below
lives in the seller's private dashboard.

### Getting started
- **Guided onboarding** — a step-by-step setup wizard that walks a new seller through creating
  their shop, so they're not staring at a blank screen.
- **Dashboard** — the "home base" showing sales, orders, and key numbers at a glance.

### Products & stock
- **Products** — add, edit, and organise everything they sell, with photos, prices, and
  variations (sizes, colours).
- **Bulk photo upload** — snap and upload many product photos at once (huge time-saver for a
  trader with hundreds of items).
- **Digital products & files** — sell downloadable goods; the system handles secure delivery.
- **Categories** — group products so the shop is easy to browse.
- **Inventory** — track how much stock is left, so they never oversell.
- **Reviews** — customers leave ratings; the seller can see and manage them.

### Orders & customers
- **Orders** — see every order, its details, and move it through stages (new → packed →
  shipped → delivered).
- **Returns** — handle customers sending items back.
- **Payments** — see which orders are paid, pending, or failed.
- **Customers** — a built-in address book of everyone who has bought, with their history (a
  simple CRM).

### Getting paid
- **Payouts** — the seller tells us where to send their money (their MoMo number or bank
  account). This is what connects their shop to real cash in their pocket.
- **Revenue & reports** — clear views of how much they're earning and selling over time.
- **Analytics PDF export** — download a tidy report to share or keep.

### Marketing & growth
- **Discounts & coupons** — run "20% off" sales or hand out promo codes.
- **Campaigns** — organised promotions to push sales.
- **Blog & recipes** — publish content that pulls in shoppers from Google and social media
  (e.g. a food seller posting recipes that use their ingredients).
- **Custom pages** — build any extra page they need.
- **Media library** — one place to manage all their images.
- **SEO dashboard** — tools that help their shop show up on Google searches, in plain-language
  controls (no tech knowledge needed).

### Look & feel
- **Themes** — pick a ready-made design for the shop.
- **Design / customisation** — tweak colours, layout, and branding so the shop looks like
  *theirs*, not a generic template.

### Shop settings
- **Settings** — the shop's name, contact info, and core preferences.
- **Store address** — where the business is located.
- **Delivery zones & rates** — set shipping fees by region (e.g. Greater Accra vs. elsewhere).
- **Suppliers & drop-shipping** — sellers can link to suppliers and even **dropship**: sell
  products that a supplier ships directly, with the money automatically split between everyone
  fairly at the moment of sale (more on this in the money engine below).
- **Verification** — sellers submit documents to get a trusted "verified" badge.

If a shop is ever paused by us (for a policy issue), the seller sees a clear **"store locked"**
page explaining why and how to fix it — never a confusing dead end.

## 3. Our World — the platform control room

This is the admin side that *we* (Makola staff) use to run the whole marketplace safely. Access
is tightly controlled: staff sign in with a password **plus** a one-time code from an
authenticator app (the same two-step security banks use), and every sensitive action is logged.

- **Platform dashboard** — the bird's-eye view of the entire business: total shops, merchants,
  orders, money flowing through.
- **Stores management** — see every shop, and **suspend, block, or archive** any of them when
  needed (e.g. a scam shop). Suspended shops go offline instantly; the model is fair and
  reversible.
- **Merchants** — manage the shop owners themselves.
- **Verifications queue** — review the documents sellers submit and approve or reject their
  "verified" badge.
- **Moderation** — review and take down products or reviews that break the rules (counterfeits,
  prohibited items), with the seller notified.
- **Announcements** — broadcast messages to sellers (e.g. "new feature launched" or "holiday
  payout schedule").
- **Onboarding oversight** — track how new sellers are progressing through setup.
- **Team** — invite and manage Makola staff, each with specific permissions (so a junior staffer
  can't touch billing, for example).
- **Security & security events** — monitor sign-ins and suspicious activity.
- **Audit log** — a permanent, tamper-resistant record of who did what and when. Essential for
  trust and accountability.
- **Impersonation ("log in as merchant")** — staff can *safely* step into a seller's account to
  help them troubleshoot, with the whole session recorded.
- **Settings** — platform-wide controls and feature switches (we can turn features on/off
  without a new release).

### The money oversight tools
- **Payments** — a cross-shop view of all transactions: success rates, failed payments to chase,
  and which payment provider is performing.
- **Refunds** — oversight of money sent back to customers across all shops.
- **Finance** — the **revenue view**: how much we've earned in fees, the total value of goods
  sold (GMV), our effective "take rate," and — crucially — a per-shop list of who we still owe
  money to and who hasn't set up their payout details yet. This is our profit-and-payout
  command centre.
- **Billing** — subscription/plan management.

## 4. The Money Engine — how cash actually moves

This is the part that makes Makola a *business*, not just software. It's worth understanding
because it's clever and it's where our revenue comes from.

### Taking payment
When a shopper pays, the money goes through a payment provider (**Paystack** or **Hubtel**) using
**mobile money** or card. Behind the scenes, a "webhook" (an instant notification from the
provider) confirms the payment so the order completes in real time, without anyone refreshing a
page.

### Splitting the money — the smart bit
Normally, when a customer pays, **all** the money would land in one big platform account, and we'd
have to pay each seller by hand later — slow and error-prone. Makola does it differently:

- **Split at the source.** At the exact moment of payment, the money is automatically divided.
  The seller's share goes straight to *their* mobile money number; **we keep only our small fee**
  — automatically, with no manual payout.
- **The platform fee.** On a normal sale, we keep a small percentage (e.g. **2%**) as our
  transaction fee. The seller gets the rest, instantly. No invoices, no chasing.
- **Drop-shipping splits three ways.** If a sale involves a supplier (drop-shipping), the system
  splits the payment automatically and fairly between the supplier, the seller, and us — all in
  one transaction, so nobody has to trust anyone to "pay them back later." This is genuinely hard
  to do and is a real competitive advantage.

### Connecting a seller to the money
- A seller enters their MoMo number on the **Payouts** page.
- The system quietly sets them up with the payment provider in the background (so saving is
  instant and never blocked by a slow network call), then marks them "ready to receive."
- Once ready, every future sale pays them directly.

### Seeing the money
- The **Finance** page (admin) shows our fees collected, total sales, and the backlog of any
  manual payouts still owed — turning "are we making money?" into a single, honest screen.

The deliberate result: **the platform earns on every transaction automatically, and sellers get
paid instantly** — exactly the model that works in markets where people don't have (or trust)
traditional banking.

## 5. Behind the Scenes — the quiet machinery

These features aren't pages people click, but they're why the platform feels fast, safe, and
professional.

- **Each shop is its own world (multi-tenancy).** Every seller's data — products, orders,
  customers — is completely walled off from every other seller's. One shop can never see or
  affect another's. This is the bedrock of trust on a shared platform.
- **Custom web addresses (subdomains).** Each shop gets its own address like
  `your-shop.makola.io`, so it feels like a real, independent website — not a stall inside someone
  else's mall.
- **WhatsApp & SMS notifications.** Order confirmations and updates reach customers where they
  actually are — on WhatsApp and SMS — not just email (which many shoppers rarely check).
- **WhatsApp login.** Both shoppers and sellers can sign in with just their phone number and a
  code. No passwords to forget — perfect for the market.
- **A mobile app foundation.** There's a secure connection (API) ready for native phone apps, plus
  push notifications, so sellers can run their shop from a dedicated app on the go.
- **Built to be found on Google (SEO).** A whole suite of behind-the-scenes optimisation —
  sitemaps, structured data, and a merchant-compatible product feed — helps search engines
  discover and understand shops. There is also an experimental `llms.txt` summary for AI tools;
  it is not a Google ranking signal and does not guarantee recommendations.
- **Background workers.** Slow jobs (sending messages, processing images, handling payment
  confirmations) happen quietly in the background, so the seller's screen never freezes waiting.
- **Security throughout.** Two-step login for staff, strict permission levels, rate-limiting to
  block abuse, safe handling of all user input, and a full audit trail. Money and trust are on the
  line, so this is treated seriously.
- **Cleanup & storage.** Product images and files are stored cost-effectively in the cloud, and
  deleted files are properly cleaned up so we don't pay to store junk.
- **Low-bandwidth everywhere.** Lazy-loaded images, light pages, minimal data use — every decision
  is made for someone on a budget phone and a patchy network.

## The big picture

Put together, Makola gives a West African entrepreneur **everything they need to sell online and
get paid** — a beautiful shop, mobile-money checkout, WhatsApp updates, marketing tools, and
instant payouts — while giving us a **safe, well-run marketplace that earns a fee on every sale**.

It's localised for how commerce *actually* works here: phones over laptops, mobile money over
credit cards, WhatsApp over email, and trust built in at every layer.
