Pricing for West African Purchasing Power

Pricing is designed for West African SME purchasing power. The Growth plan at GHS 49/mo ($3.16) is less than the cost of a single social media ad in Ghana. This positions Emakola as an accessible step up from free tools without the sticker shock of Western platforms like Shopify (GHS 600+/mo).

Gateway Fees Are Pass-Through

Payment gateway fees (~GHS 200 / ~$12.90) are charged directly to the merchant by Paystack or Hubtel. These do not flow through Emakola's P&L, resulting in the true operating cost of ~GHS 65 (~$4.19) per merchant and an 85% gross margin.

Above-Benchmark Unit Economics

Emakola's short payback period (<2 months vs. industry 5-12 months) is driven by low CAC through organic and community-based acquisition channels prevalent in West African markets. The transaction fee model contributes to net revenue retention above 110% as merchants grow their GMV on the platform.

Pricing Strategy Rationale

Strategic Positioning
  • GHS 49/mo is approximately 1% of target merchant monthly revenue (GHS 5,000+), making it a no-brainer investment for growth-stage merchants.
  • Positioned in a clear gap between free tools (Paystack Commerce, Flutterwave Store) and expensive Western platforms (Shopify at GHS 600+/mo).
  • Transaction fee model aligns Emakola's success directly with merchant success — when they grow, Emakola grows.
  • Free tier creates an acquisition funnel that builds merchant dependency on the platform before upselling to paid tiers with advanced features.
At GHS 49/mo, a merchant selling just GHS 2,450 worth of goods per month recovers the subscription cost through efficiency gains alone. That is less than 100 orders of GHS 25 each — well within reach for even a small Accra market vendor. Internal merchant economics analysis, 2026
