Section 06

Marketing & Growth Strategy

Acquiring, activating, and retaining West African merchants through high-trust, low-cost channels built around community, partnerships, and social proof.

Retention Strategy

Acquiring merchants is only half the battle. Our retention playbook is built around proactive engagement via WhatsApp (the channel merchants already live on), automated nudges, and a dedicated success team for high-value accounts.

Merchant Lifecycle — First 30 Days

Day 1
Guided Onboarding Wizard
3 steps: name your store, add your first product, go live. Designed to get a merchant operational in under 10 minutes.
Day 3
"Add Your First 5 Products" Prompt
WhatsApp message with tips on product photography and writing descriptions that sell. Links to Twi-language tutorial video.
Day 7
First Sale Celebration or Help Offer
If the merchant has made a sale: celebration message with stats. If not: personal outreach offering setup help.
Day 14
Analytics Review
"You had X visitors this week" — WhatsApp summary with actionable insights on traffic sources and top products.
Day 30
Feature Upsell
If on Free tier: highlight Growth plan features relevant to their usage. "You're getting X orders/week — unlock analytics and custom domain."
Ongoing
Monthly Merchant Success Report
WhatsApp-delivered monthly summary: revenue, orders, top products, growth vs. previous month, actionable tips.

Churn Prevention Triggers

No Login — 7 Days

Automated WhatsApp nudge: "Your store had X visitors while you were away. Log in to check your dashboard."

Trigger: No admin session for 7+ days

No Orders — 14 Days

Success team personal outreach via WhatsApp. Offer help with product listings, pricing strategy, or sharing store link.

Trigger: Zero orders for 14+ days

Payment Expiring

SMS + WhatsApp reminder when payment method is about to expire. Include one-tap link to update billing.

Trigger: 7 days before payment method expiry

Brand Positioning

"Your shop, your rules." — Emakola's brand promise is independence. Independence from marketplaces that take 20% and own your customers. Independence from WhatsApp chaos. Independence from platforms priced for Silicon Valley, not Accra. We don't just give merchants a store — we give them ownership. Emakola Brand Promise
Competitive Positioning

Emakola occupies a unique position: affordable like free tools, powerful like enterprise platforms, local like nothing else. We compete not by matching Shopify feature-for-feature, but by deeply understanding the West African merchant's workflow — mobile money payments, WhatsApp-first communication, low-bandwidth optimization, and pricing in cedis, not dollars.

Virality Mechanics

Every Emakola store is a marketing channel. Our virality strategy embeds organic distribution into the product itself, turning every merchant and every checkout into a potential acquisition event.

"Powered by Emakola" Badge

Every storefront displays a tasteful "Powered by Emakola" badge in the footer. This provides free advertising at scale — as merchants grow, so does our brand visibility.

Removable on Pro and Enterprise plans.

Shareable Store Links

Store links optimized for WhatsApp and Instagram sharing — rich link previews with store logo, description, and featured product image. One tap to share, one tap to visit.

Merchant Success Stories

Monthly spotlight on successful merchants across social media and the Emakola blog. Real stories, real numbers — social proof that drives organic sign-ups from peers.

Checkout Experience as Marketing

A checkout experience so smooth — mobile money in 2 taps, instant WhatsApp confirmation — that customers ask merchants: "What platform is this?" Every great checkout is a referral opportunity.
