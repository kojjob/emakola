Financial Inclusion

Across West Africa, millions of small merchants operate entirely in cash with no digital footprint. Emakola bridges this gap by making digital commerce accessible without traditional banking infrastructure.

Mobile Money Access

Enabling merchants who are unbanked or underbanked to accept digital payments through mobile money — no bank account required. MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo Money are supported natively.

Formalization

Merchants on Emakola get sales records, tax-ready transaction logs, and business analytics — formalizing informal businesses and creating a documented trading history.

Credit Readiness

Transaction history on the platform can serve as alternative credit data for merchant financing. Consistent sales records unlock future access to microloans and working capital products.

Cross-border Payments

Enabling merchants to sell beyond their immediate geography. As Emakola expands to Nigeria, merchants gain access to a 220M-person market without payment friction.

Gender Impact

Designing for Women Entrepreneurs

  • Women own approximately 44% of SMEs in Ghana, one of the highest rates globally
  • Fashion, beauty, and food — dominant categories on Instagram commerce — are predominantly women-led businesses
  • Emakola's primary target persona (Ama — The Instagram Seller) is a woman entrepreneur running her business from her phone
  • By designing for mobile-first, WhatsApp-native workflows, Emakola is inherently designing for women entrepreneurs who disproportionately use these tools
Target: 60%+ of Emakola merchants will be women-led businesses

SDG Alignment

Emakola's mission aligns directly with six United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. Impact is not a side effect — it is the business model.

SDG 1
No Poverty
Enabling income generation for micro and small businesses. Every merchant on Emakola gains access to digital tools that expand their customer base and revenue potential.
SDG 5
Gender Equality
Empowering women entrepreneurs who represent 44% of Ghana's SMEs. Mobile-first design and WhatsApp-native workflows are built for how women merchants already operate.
SDG 8
Decent Work & Economic Growth
Creating direct employment and enabling entrepreneurship. From tech jobs in Accra to rider employment in the delivery marketplace, Emakola is a job creation engine.
SDG 9

Building digital commerce infrastructure for West Africa. Emakola provides the rails for ecommerce in a region where Shopify and similar platforms fail to serve.

ESG Considerations for Investors

Emakola meets the Environmental, Social, and Governance criteria that impact-focused and institutional investors increasingly require.

E Environmental
  • SaaS model — minimal physical footprint
  • Digital receipts reduce paper waste across thousands of transactions
  • Consolidating delivery routes reduces emissions vs. individual merchant logistics
  • Fly.io uses green energy data centers for hosting
S Social
  • Merchant empowerment and business formalization
  • Financial inclusion via mobile money integration
  • Gender equity in entrepreneurship (60%+ target)
  • Youth employment pipeline through multiple pathways
  • Digital literacy programs through merchant onboarding
G Governance
  • Data Protection Act compliance (Ghana)
  • Transparent pricing — no hidden fees
  • Merchant data ownership — merchants can export all data
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Anti-fraud systems for payment protection
Impact investors increasingly seek "commercially sustainable" impact — businesses where financial returns and social impact are inseparable. Emakola is one such business: every dollar of revenue requires a merchant succeeding, every merchant succeeding means a small business growing, and every small business growing means jobs and economic participation in West Africa.
