Data Quality Notes

Where multiple sources disagree (particularly on ecommerce market sizing), both figures are included with their methodologies noted. Glassdoor salary data for Ghana has small sample sizes and should be used directionally. Africa-specific SaaS benchmarks do not exist in published form — global benchmarks serve as proxies with adjustments for market conditions. All data reflects the most recent available as of March 2026.
