Appendix C

Glossary

Key terms, abbreviations, and local context

Currency & Financial Terms

TermDefinition
GHSGhanaian Cedi (GH₵) — official currency of Ghana. ISO 4217 code: GHS.
PesewasMinor unit of the Ghana Cedi. 1 GHS = 100 pesewas. All monetary amounts in Emakola are stored in pesewas to avoid floating-point errors.
NGNNigerian Naira (₦) — official currency of Nigeria.
KoboMinor unit of the Nigerian Naira. 1 NGN = 100 kobo.
XOFWest African CFA Franc — used in 8 Francophone West African countries (Senegal, Ivory Coast, etc.).
GMVGross Merchandise Value — total value of goods sold through the platform before fees and returns.
MRRMonthly Recurring Revenue — predictable subscription revenue per month.
ARRAnnual Recurring Revenue — MRR x 12.
ARPUAverage Revenue Per User (merchant) — total revenue divided by active merchants.
CACCustomer Acquisition Cost — total marketing spend divided by new merchants acquired.
LTVLifetime Value — predicted total revenue from a merchant over their entire relationship with Emakola.
EBITDAEarnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization.
NPSNet Promoter Score — customer satisfaction metric (-100 to +100).

Payment & Mobile Money

TermDefinition
MoMo / MTN MoMoMTN Mobile Money — the largest mobile money service in Ghana with ~70%+ market share. Allows sending/receiving money, bill payments, and merchant payments via phone.
Telecel CashTelecel's mobile money service in Ghana. ~20% market share.
AirtelTigo MoneyAirtelTigo's mobile money service in Ghana. ~10% market share.
USSDUnstructured Supplementary Service Data — a protocol used by mobile money (e.g., dialing *170#). Works on any phone, no internet required.
PaystackNigerian fintech company (acquired by Stripe in 2020) that provides payment processing APIs for African businesses. Handles cards, bank transfers, mobile money.
HubtelGhanaian fintech company providing mobile money payment APIs, SMS, and merchant services.
FlutterwaveNigerian fintech company ($3B valuation) providing pan-African payment infrastructure.
CODCash on Delivery — customer pays the rider in cash when the order arrives. Requires a confirmation workflow.
E-LevyElectronic Transaction Levy — Ghana government tax on electronic transfers (currently 1-1.5%).
GhIPSSGhana Interbank Payment and Settlement Systems — manages mobile money interoperability.

Technology

TermDefinition
ElixirFunctional programming language built on the Erlang VM (BEAM). Known for concurrency, fault tolerance, and low-latency real-time applications.
PhoenixWeb framework for Elixir. Comparable to Rails (Ruby) or Django (Python) but with real-time capabilities built in.
LiveViewPhoenix library for building real-time, server-rendered UIs without JavaScript. Sends DOM diffs over WebSocket — ~30KB payload vs 300KB+ for React SPAs.
Ash FrameworkElixir framework for building domain-driven applications with declarative resources, built-in multi-tenancy, and authorization policies.
OTPOpen Telecom Platform — Erlang's framework for building fault-tolerant, distributed systems. Supervision trees automatically restart failed processes.
BEAMBogdan/Bjorn's Erlang Abstract Machine — the virtual machine that runs Elixir and Erlang. Designed for massive concurrency and fault tolerance.
ObanElixir library for background job processing. Used for payment webhooks, notifications, and image processing.
Multi-tenancyArchitecture pattern where a single application serves multiple customers (tenants/stores) with data isolation between them.
PWAProgressive Web App — web application that can be installed on a phone like a native app, with offline support.
3GThird-generation mobile network. Still the dominant connection type outside major African cities. Typical speeds: 300kbps-1Mbps.

Business & Regulatory

TermDefinition
SMESmall and Medium Enterprise. In Ghana: businesses with 6-100 employees. SMEs represent 92% of all Ghanaian businesses.
MSMEMicro, Small and Medium Enterprise. Includes sole proprietors and micro-businesses with fewer than 6 employees.
TAMTotal Addressable Market — the total revenue opportunity for a product/service if 100% market share were achieved.
SAMServiceable Available Market — the portion of TAM that can be reached with the current business model and geographic focus.
SOMServiceable Obtainable Market — the realistic portion of SAM that can be captured in the near term.
Ghana DPAGhana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) — governs collection, processing, and storage of personal data. Requires registration with the Data Protection Commission.
Nigeria NDPANigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 — comprehensive data protection law replacing the earlier NDPR.
VATValue Added Tax — 15% standard rate in Ghana on goods and services.
NHILNational Health Insurance Levy — 2.5% levy on goods and services in Ghana.
GETFundGhana Education Trust Fund levy — 2.5% levy on goods and services in Ghana.
AGIAssociation of Ghana Industries — business association for manufacturers and service providers.
NBSSINational Board for Small Scale Industries — Ghana government agency supporting SME development (now part of Ghana Enterprises Agency).
PCI DSSPayment Card Industry Data Security Standard — security standard for organizations handling card payments.
SDGSustainable Development Goals — 17 goals adopted by the United Nations in 2015 as a blueprint for sustainable development.
ESGEnvironmental, Social, and Governance — framework for evaluating a company's impact beyond financial performance.

Ghana-Specific Terms

TermDefinition
Ghana CardNational identification card issued by the National Identification Authority (NIA). Required for KYC, mobile money registration, and rider onboarding.
OsuVibrant commercial district in Accra, known for Oxford Street shopping. High concentration of small retail businesses.
Makola MarketLargest open-air market in Accra. Symbol of Ghana's informal commerce economy.
TwiMost widely spoken local language in Ghana (Akan language). Content strategy includes Twi for merchant onboarding and tutorials.
Tecno / InfinixChinese smartphone brands (by Transsion) that dominate the African market with affordable devices under $50-150.
KenteTraditional Ghanaian woven cloth, often used as a symbol of Ghanaian identity and craftsmanship.
