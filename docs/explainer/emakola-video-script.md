# Emakola — Explainer Video Script & Production Bible

> Companion to `emakola-explainer.html`. A production-ready script for a short, sound‑off,
> WhatsApp‑first explainer video that anyone — buyer, trader, wholesaler, or owner — can
> understand in 90 seconds.

**Logline.** Three people share three fears about trading online in Ghana — *"I have no money to start," "Will they pay me?," "Will I get my goods?"* — and Emakola erases all three at once: the bank splits every cedi **instantly and fairly**, like the market queen (*konkohemaa*) who hands each person their share on the spot.

**Primary format:** 9:16 vertical · **90‑second master** · burned‑in (open) captions · must read fully with **sound off**.
**Cutdowns:** 30s (WhatsApp Status / Reels) · 15s (teaser). 16:9 horizontal master for YouTube / site embed.

---

##  The non‑negotiables (read before filming)

1. **The 70 / 28 / 2 money‑split is the hero** — it gets the single largest time block (~12s of a 90s film).
2. **100% understandable with sound off.** Voiceover is a bonus layer, never load‑bearing.
3. **Every cedi figure appears as large on‑screen text** (₵100, ₵70, ₵28, ₵2) — never spoken‑only.
4. **Actor = colour, always the same:** Buyer = emerald `#0c5c44` · Wholesaler = slate `#6b7280` · Trader = emerald‑deep · Emakola = charcoal `#1b1e20`. (Matches the explainer's money‑split diagram exactly.)
5. **Captions ≤ 6 words per card**, held ≥ 1.5s (≥ 2.5s for any card with a number).

##  Sound‑off / low‑literacy rules

- **≤ 6 words per caption** (aim 3–4); one idea per card; dwell ≥ 1.5s (≥ 2.5s with a number).
- **Burned‑in captions** (not soft subtitles — WhatsApp Status won't render them). Keep the top 12% and bottom 12% clear for platform UI.
- **High contrast only:** charcoal `#23272a` on off-white `#f6f5f2`, or white on emerald `#0c5c44`. Body text stays charcoal; use the emerald or jade accent for emphasis.
- **Money is a physical, moving object** — coins/notes/MoMo balances literally splitting and flying to three labelled pockets. No abstract "transaction" graphics.
- **Slow is correct.** ~1 new idea every 3–4 seconds; stagger the three arrows so the eye follows each share, *then* snap all three for "same second."
- **Acceptance test:** mute it, shrink to a 360px‑wide phone, hand it to someone who reads slowly. If they can tell you *"Ama paid ₵100, it split into ₵70/₵28/₵2 to three people at once, and nobody can steal it"* — it works.

| Block | 90s master | Job |
|-------|-----------|-----|

| The promise | 8s | One line: what Emakola is + the four people. |
| ** The split** | **~22s (4+5)** | The whole reason the video exists. Biggest block. |

| Happy ending | 12s | Payoff: shipped, received, everyone paid. |
| CTA | 10s | One concrete action. |

##  Music & voice

- **Music:** warm, communal Ghanaian highlife / Afrobeats‑lite — light percussion, kalimba/guitar, ~90–110 BPM. Lifts at the split and the ending. **Not** corporate fintech, **not** Western EDM. Everything legible at 50% phone‑speaker volume.
- **Voice:** **Ghanaian English**, warm and conversational — a trusted neighbour, not a slick announcer. Short sentences, real terms (cedis, MoMo, market). Leave silence at the split.
- **Signature sounds:** a satisfying "coin/chime" exactly when the money snaps into the three pockets, and a "phone buzz" on each alert — but the film must read fully without them.

##  How to produce it (ranked by cost)

1. **Recommended — animate the existing explainer + voiceover.** The money‑split SVG, palette (`#0c5c44` / `#1f9d6b` / `#23272a` / `#f6f5f2`), fonts, and actor icons already exist in `emakola-explainer.html`. Export the SVG arrows + ₵ numbers and animate them (After Effects / Rive / Lottie — cleaner than raw scroll‑capture). Add MoMo‑prompt and WhatsApp‑bubble mockups. ~1–3 days. Cheapest, perfectly on‑brand.
2. **Purpose‑built 2D motion graphics** from the same design tokens + the kente divider as a transition. ~4–7 days; most polished hero beat.
3. **Live‑action market shoot** (real Ama/Kwame/Akosua, real market) with the split rendered as a graphics overlay. Warmest, most authentic; most time/cost — best as a v2 once the message is proven cheaply with option 1.

##  Do not

-  Generic "Africa rising" / sunset‑acacia / running‑children stock footage — show a *specific* Ghanaian market and real phones.
-  Fast Silicon‑Valley fintech montage / neon dashboards / glassy 3D cards.
-  Admin‑UI screen‑recordings as the hero — the **split** is the hero, not the product UI.
-  Text walls, captions under 1.2s, or low‑contrast text.
-  Spoken‑only numbers or punchlines that need sound.
-  Jargon on screen ("subaccount", "settlement", "split‑payment API") — plain words only.
-  Animating all three arrows at once on the first reveal — stagger, then snap.
-  Changing an actor's colour mid‑video. Buyer = emerald · Wholesaler = slate · Trader = jade · Emakola = charcoal.
